Indian

Butter Chicken

A creamy and flavorful Indian chicken curry made with tomato, butter and aromatic spices.

⭐ 4.8 / 5
45 min Cooking Time
4 Servings
Medium Difficulty

🧂 Ingredients

  • 500g chicken
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tomatoes
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup fresh cream
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • Salt as required
  • Fresh coriander

👨‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

1

Wash the chicken and marinate it with turmeric, chilli powder, salt and ginger-garlic paste.

2

Heat butter in a pan and cook the marinated chicken until lightly browned.

3

Add chopped onions and cook until they become soft and golden brown.

4

Add tomato puree and cook the mixture until the oil starts separating from the masala.

5

Add the cooked chicken and garam masala. Mix everything well.

6

Add fresh cream and simmer on low heat for about 5 minutes.

7

Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot with rice, naan or roti.

💡 Cooking Tip: Cook the tomato masala properly before adding the cream. This gives the curry a richer flavor.
